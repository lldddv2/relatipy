# data/

Real input data (copied from the user's uploads, byte-for-byte) and the generated mocks.

## s2_astrometry_gravity2020_fig1.csv

- Provenance: user upload `99bedce8-gravity2020_fig1_astrometry_full.csv` (sha256
  `e28b87e7…27d0f2`). S2 astrometry from Fig. 1 of GRAVITY 2020 (going by the file name, presumably
  GRAVITY Collaboration 2020, A&A 636 L5),
  1992.22 to 2019.74. Astrometry only (no radial velocities).
- Columns: `t_yr` (decimal year), `ra_mas`, `ra_err_mas`, `dec_mas`, `dec_err_mas`, `instrument`.
  RA offset positive to the east, Dec positive to the north, relative to Sgr A*; errors are 1-sigma
  per coordinate in mas.
- Instruments: `NACO/SHARP`, 128 rows (= the 128 NACO epochs of GRAVITY 2024), errors 0.3 to 5 mas;
  `GRAVITY`, 57 rows, errors 0.02 to 0.4 mas.
- Flags and known issues:
  - Two GRAVITY rows are exact duplicates (2017.2396800 and 2017.5022380, each listed twice).
    `schedules.load_s2_csv()` drops them by default, which leaves 55 unique GRAVITY epochs.
  - 2002.6597 and 2002.6598 are two distinct NACO rows 9 h apart. Both are kept.
  - The NACO points carry the NACO reference frame (offset plus drift relative to Sgr A*, roughly
    0.1 to 1 mas and 0.01 to 0.1 mas/yr). The GRAVITY points are referenced to Sgr A* directly. Fits
    to the real NACO data need the frame parameters (see `validation.md`).

## s301_radec_digitized_nature2026_fig.csv

- Provenance: user upload `637323a2-s301_radec_digitized.csv` (sha256 `673a754b…59f7`). S301 sky
  positions digitized from the vector PDF of the S301 discovery figure (GRAVITY Collaboration 2026,
  Nature, arXiv:2607.12664). `manual_review = pending` for all rows.
- Columns: `point_id`, `year_digitized` (decimal year read from the figure), `ra_mas`, `dec_mas`,
  `ra_errorbar_halfwidth_mas`, `dec_errorbar_halfwidth_mas` (half-widths of the plotted error bars),
  `provenance` (`digitized_vector_pdf`), `manual_review` (`pending`).
- 20 points. 19 of them match, within 0.7 d, the 19 GRAVITY epochs of Extended Data Table 1
  (2017.2389 to 2025.5978). `schedules.load_s301_digitized()` replaces their digitized years with the
  table epochs and sets `flag = matched`.
- Point 7 (2023.577267) has no Extended Data Table 1 epoch, so it gets `flag = unmatched_2023.577`. It
  is kept in the file but excluded by default (`ScheduleConfig.s301_include_unmatched = False`). Its
  residual is ordinary (about 0.55 mas in Dec, like its neighbours; `validation.md`), so it may be a
  real plotted point missing from the table, or a misread symbol.
- Errors: the paper gives 207 µas per coordinate for every epoch (the pixel-grid noise floor, Methods
  p.19), and the schedules use that value. The digitized half-widths (0.02 to 1.6 mas) are
  figure-level and partly digitization artefacts (for example 0.02 mas in RA at 2024.2325). Do not
  use them as errors.
- No radial velocities exist for S301 (paper p.5).
