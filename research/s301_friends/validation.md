# Validation of the default forward model

Generated by `validate.py` (relatipy KerrMcmcModel.get_ra_dec_vr, chi = 0 unless stated, dop853 rtol 1e-11). Residual = data - model. RMS per coordinate in mas.

## 1. Angle and sign conventions

- S29 within +-3 yr of its 1977 osculation epoch (near apocentre, r ~ 1.5e5 r_g): relatipy vs a hand-written Kepler orbit in the Gillessen+09 convention (x = Dec north, y = RA east, z = away, Omega from north through east, ascending node = receding): max |d(RA, Dec)| = 70.08 uas (Romer delay + 1PN); v_los - dz/dt = 2.14 .. 2.16 km/s (gravitational redshift + transverse Doppler ~ (3/2) GM/(rc) ~ +3 km/s). Same signs and frame: relatipy alpha = +RA (east), delta = +Dec (north), v > 0 receding.
- S2 line-of-sight velocity at 2018.30 / 2018.38 / 2018.45: +4065 / +2486 / -7 km/s (receding at pericentre, then the steep drop, as the published S2 RV curve).
- Published elements (both GRAVITY 2024 Table B.1 and arXiv:2607.12664, which states 'angle conventions follow Gillessen+09') can be passed to relatipy unchanged: no 180 deg shift is needed. (Foschi+ arXiv:2607.26713 Eq. 42 uses Omega_obs = Omega_GRAV + pi, omega_obs = omega_GRAV + pi, i.e. a z-flipped frame; do not mix those.)
- Spin vectors are given to relatipy in the same observer axes (Dec, RA, away); the orbital pole is L = (sin O sin i, -cos O sin i, cos i).

- A vs B sky tracks (chi = 0, paper M/R0), 2017-2041: max separation 0.57 mas; away from the 2023 pericentre (|t - t_p| > 0.3 yr) median 253 uas. v_los: A +7601 vs B -7200 km/s at 2031.81; max |v_A + v_B| = 3177 km/s vs max |v| = 17580 km/s (z-mirror: RV flips sign; the difference is the different e, i and the even redshift terms).
- Osculation epoch: the published elements reproduce the figure only if they are osculating BEFORE the 2023.13 pericentre (any epoch 2017-2022.9: chi2 within ~3 of each other). Osculating after pericentre gives RMS ~3 mas: across pericentre the osculating omega jumps by the Schwarzschild advance (~2 deg), i.e. ~3 mas at 83 mas. Default: preceding apocentre t_p - P/2 = 2018.785, as GRAVITY 2024 for S2/S29.
- An initial state AT pericentre (e = 0.983) would be the worst choice in general: there the Kepler (a, e) <-> state map is ill-conditioned at 1PN (E ~ (1 - e) GM/r_p while v^2/c^2 ~ 0.7%).
- With the default epoch the RMS is 0.23 mas in RA and 0.47 mas in Dec (chi2 ~120 for 38 numbers at 207 uas, against the published 25.9 / 25.5). The excess is mostly in Dec and in single points (2023.26, 2023.43, 2023.51, 2024.33, 2024.41, 2025.52 at 0.5-1 mas), which points to digitization error or figure-level differences, not to the elements. The digitized error-bar half-widths (0.02-1.6 mas) are not the 207 uas of the paper (e.g. 0.02 mas in RA at 2024.2325 is a digitization artefact); the schedules use 207 uas. Good enough to confirm elements, epochs and conventions, not for a refit; manual review of the digitized file is still pending.
- The unmatched 2023.577 point lies on the orbit (residual ~0.55 mas in Dec, like its neighbours), so it is probably a real plotted point without an Extended Data Table 1 entry (or a duplicated/misread symbol); it stays excluded by default.
