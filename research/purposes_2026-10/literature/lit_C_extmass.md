## Q1. Extended mass / dark matter inside S2 orbit  [sources: WebSearch snippets; primary abstracts not fetchable]
Functional forms used in the literature (from snippets): Plummer profile (scale a = 0.3") and power-law rho ~ r^-gamma (GRAVITY 2020 App. D); Plummer and power-law (GRAVITY 2022, 2024); Bahcall-Wolf cusp and Plummer (Heissel+2022 slides); NFW(gamma=1)+Gondolo-Silk spike gamma_sp=7/3 (Lacroix 2018); gNFW and Einasto (alpha=0.17) spikes (Shen+2024). Spike slope 7/3 for an initial gamma=1 cusp follows from Gondolo & Silk 1999 formula gamma_sp=(9-2gamma)/(4-gamma) (formula from memory: unverified against the paper).

- GRAVITY Collab. 2020, "Detection of the Schwarzschild precession in the orbit of the star S2 near the Galactic centre massive black hole", A&A 636, L5; arXiv:2004.07187. Appendix D (snippet): extended mass inside S2 orbit ~0.1% of M_BH, i.e. ~4000 Msun (1 sigma). Plummer a=0.3": fraction 0.00 +/- 0.10%; power law: -0.03 +/- 0.07%. Stellar mass inside S2 apocentre judged <1000 Msun; stellar BHs 80-340 Msun. Fit: 14 parameters incl. distance, M_BH, GR-precession term (snippet). Model/integrator: GR (Schwarzschild) precession fit; exact integrator unverified.
- GRAVITY Collab. 2022, "Mass distribution in the Galactic Center based on interferometric astrometry of multiple stellar orbits", A&A 657, L12 (published online Jan 2022); arXiv:2112.07478 (first posted Dec 2021). Snippet-level: 1 sigma ceiling ~3000 Msun on extended mass inside S2 apocentre (~0.23" ~ 0.1% M_BH) under plausible density profiles; precession significance ~7 sigma; M_BH quoted ~4.30e6 Msun (value unverified). Data through mid-2021. Model: unverified (text suggests GR precession fit; not confirmed).
- GRAVITY Collab. 2024, "Improving constraints on the extended mass distribution in the Galactic Center with stellar orbits", A&A 692, A242 (snippet); arXiv:2409.12261. S2 + S29 + S38 + S55 near pericentre; prograde in-plane precession at ~10 sigma. Power-law + Plummer fits: mass enclosed by S2 orbit compatible with zero; 1 sigma upper limit ~1200 Msun; steeper power-law (gamma <~ -1.2) gives tighter bound (snippet). Authors claim it sits near stellar-cusp expectations, so no significant DM spike. Fit model Newtonian vs Schwarzschild: NOT stated in snippets (unverified; check Sect. 3).
- Heissel et al. 2022, "The dark mass signature in the orbit of S2", A&A 660, A13 (authors per snippets: G. Heissel, T. Paumard, G. Perrin, F. Vincent; LESIA). arXiv:2112.07778. NB: the query author list (Habibi, Pfuhl) is NOT correct per the slide/abstract snippets. Method: single-orbit analysis; mass-driven drift concentrated in apocentre half vs relativistic drift peaking near pericentre, so the two are separable (snippet). Cited prior limit ~4000 Msun (1 sigma, GRAVITY 2020). Own new numerical limit: unverified. Numerical integration with OOGRE (orbital elements of a perturbed Kepler problem, per slides).

My arithmetic (not from a source). Approximate S2 elements: a = 1029 AU, e = 0.884, i = 134.5 deg, M = 4.3e6 Msun, chi = 1 (Kerr Q=-M a^2 mapped to J2 R^2 = a^2). Formulas: GR periapse 6 pi GM/(c^2 p); quadrupole (J2-type) periapse 2 pi (3/4)(4-5 sin^2 i)(a_s/p)^2 with a_s = chi GM/c^2; Lense-Thirring node Omega-dot = 2GJ/(c^2 a^3 (1-e^2)^1.5), periapse omega-dot = -6GJ cos i/(c^2 a^3 (1-e^2)^1.5), J = chi G M^2/c. Results (chi=1, per orbit, P = 15.9 yr):
  GR periapse ~ 734 arcsec (~12.2 arcmin; consistent with GRAVITY 2020's ~12'/orbit)
  Quadrupole ~ 50 mas per orbit (~3.2 mas/yr); quadrupole/GR ~ 7e-5
  LT node ~ 6.7 arcsec per orbit (~0.42 arcsec/yr); LT node/GR ~ 9e-3
  LT periapse ~ 14 arcsec per orbit; ~2% of GR
  For chi = 0.5: quadrupole ~13 mas/orbit, LT node ~3.4 arcsec/orbit.
  So for S2 the spin (LT) signal is ~1% of GR per orbit and ~130x the quadrupole signal. GRAVITY 2020 scaling factor of the GR precession, 1.10 +/- 0.19 (snippet), is ~19% precise, so spin is not yet resolvable with S2 alone (my inference).
  Caveat: my per-orbit numbers depend on approximate elements; check before quoting.
- Cluster masking summary (sources + inference): quadrupole needs ~<0.2 mpc orbits (Merritt+2010); frame dragging swamped beyond ~0.5 mpc (Merritt+2010); Sadeghian & Will 2011 find GR spin+quadrupole dominate for close stars. Per-orbit magnitudes for a generic cluster are not given in a usable form in the snippets (unverified).

- Is S2 a geodesic? Statements that the retrieved sources support:
  * GRAVITY 2020 (arXiv:2004.07187): GR Schwarzschild precession detected; GR scaling factor 1.10 +/- 0.19 (snippet), i.e. consistent with the geodesic prediction at ~19%.
  * GRAVITY 2022 (arXiv:2112.07478): 7 sigma precession; extended mass inside S2 apocentre < ~3000 Msun (1 sigma), ~7e-4 of M_BH = 4.3e6 Msun (my arithmetic).
  * GRAVITY 2024 (arXiv:2409.12261): ~10 sigma precession; enclosed mass < ~1200 Msun (1 sigma), ~2.8e-4 of M_BH (my arithmetic); smooth power-law/Plummer models.
  * Consequence (my inference): S2 is consistent with a Schwarzschild geodesic at the ~19% level on the GR precession amplitude, and at ~3e-4 M_BH on smooth mass perturbations inside its orbit. No retrieved source bounds drag, tides, winds or IMBH effects on S2 quantitatively.
